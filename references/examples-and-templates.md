## More Industry Examples

**Early-stage Startup (Product-Market Fit)**  
- CSFs: Finding repeatable acquisition channel; Achieving strong retention after activation; Building a product users love enough to recommend  
- OKR example: Increase activated users who invite ≥1 other person from 8% → 25%  
- KPIs: Activation rate, invite rate, organic vs paid mix, NPS or product-market fit score

**Scale-up (Series B/C)**  
- CSFs: Efficient growth (LTV:CAC); Operational excellence / unit economics; Leadership bench strength  
- OKR example: Improve LTV:CAC from 2.8x → 4.0x while growing ARR 80%+  
- KPIs: LTV:CAC, payback period, gross margin, eNPS / manager effectiveness scores

**Enterprise / Mature Company**  
- CSFs: Customer trust & brand strength; Innovation pipeline that converts; Cost discipline without killing growth  
- OKR example: Launch 2 new products that each reach $10M ARR run-rate within 12 months of GA  
- KPIs: Brand consideration scores, % revenue from products <3 years old, operating margin, innovation conversion rate

**Internal Team (e.g. Engineering)**  
- CSFs: System reliability; Developer productivity & happiness; Shipping business-critical features on time  
- OKR example: Reduce P0/P1 incidents by 40% and keep change failure rate <15%  
- KPIs: MTTR, change failure rate, deployment frequency, developer satisfaction / eNPS

**Marketing**  
- CSFs: Pipeline contribution quality; Brand that compounds; Efficient spend  
- OKR example: Increase marketing-sourced pipeline that converts to closed-won by 35%  
- KPIs: MQL → SQL → Opportunity conversion, CAC by channel, share of voice, content engagement quality scores

## Cascade Example (full)

**Company CSF**: Become the default choice for mid-market teams that need [core value]

↓

**Company OKR**:  
Objective: Make the product the clear category leader for mid-market  
- KR: Increase mid-market win rate vs top 2 competitors from 31% → 48%  
- KR: Raise unaided brand awareness in target segment from 12% → 28%  
- KR: Achieve 40+ NPS among mid-market customers

↓

**Supporting Team OKRs / KPIs**  
Sales: Win-rate KPIs + competitive displacement rate  
Product: Feature parity + superiority scores on top 5 buying criteria  
Marketing: Share of voice + consideration lift  
CS: Mid-market NPS + expansion rate
